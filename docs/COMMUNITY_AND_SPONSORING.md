# Community, discussions and sponsorship

## Community purpose

This is a small, public, MIT-licensed engineering showcase, not a commercial lottery product or public research archive. The goal is a technically credible example of maintainable, inexpensive GitHub governance and automation.

Use [Discussions](https://github.com/lxcoding95/jackpot-atlas-showcase/discussions) for questions, ideas and suggestions; use [Issues](https://github.com/lxcoding95/jackpot-atlas-showcase/issues) for reproducible bugs or explicitly scoped work; use [Pull Requests](https://github.com/lxcoding95/jackpot-atlas-showcase/pulls) for code or documentation changes.

Be respectful and relevant. Do not post abusive material, spam, unrelated advertising, private source, identifying information, secrets, vulnerability details, licensed third-party datasets or claims of guaranteed lottery winnings. Maintainers may moderate or close conversations. Automated checks cannot guarantee abuse-free discussions or replace an accountable human moderator.

## Responsible public statements

A fair, independent lottery drawing does not become more predictable from historical frequencies or AI-generated numbers. Do not present any showcase automation, workflow badge, or public repository as evidence of enhanced jackpot winning chances.

## Optional funding, not a payment feature

The repository's GitHub **Sponsorships** setting may be enabled, but that does **not** establish an active recipient or payment destination. Add `.github/FUNDING.yml` only after the repository owner has independently confirmed a real, eligible funding account and deliberately approved its exact URL/username. Do not use placeholder handles or redirect sponsorship to a paid lottery service.

GitHub Sponsors onboarding, financial identity, taxes and payout approval belong to the account owner. No fee, bank account, subscription, product entitlement or donation destination is operated by this repository.

Official setup guidance: https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/displaying-a-sponsor-button-in-your-repository
